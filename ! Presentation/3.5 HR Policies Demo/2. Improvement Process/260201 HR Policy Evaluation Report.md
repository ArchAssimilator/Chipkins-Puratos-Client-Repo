# HR Policy Evaluation Report

Client: Chipkins Puratos (South Africa)
Date: 1 February 2026

## 1. Executive summary

The current HR policy set is not collectively exhaustive and contains overlaps. The most material compliance gaps relate to harassment (including sexual harassment), disciplinary and grievance procedures, core conditions of employment, employment equity and anti-discrimination, data protection, and whistleblowing. Several existing policies reference other documents that are not present in the provided folder, and there are duplicates and internal inconsistencies that could create uneven application and legal risk.

Priority actions are to consolidate overlapping policies, introduce a harassment/violence prevention policy aligned to the Employment Equity Code, implement core employee relations policies (disciplinary, grievance, and conditions of employment), and establish POPIA-compliant privacy and records handling procedures. A structured remediation programme is recommended to close statutory gaps and align with international best practice.

## 3. Assessment of current policies vs. checklist

### 3.1 Policy inventory (title and scope)
- **ALCOHOL & DRUGS REGULATION POLICY**: Zero-tolerance alcohol/drug policy; random testing; breathalyser/lab testing; disciplinary consequences; applies to all employees.
- **DRUG AND ALCOHOL POLICY**: Detailed definitions and procedures, high-risk roles, testing protocols, consent/representation, pre-employment testing, work-related function scope.
- **HEALTH AND SAFETY IN THE WORKPLACE**: OHSA overview, employer/employee responsibilities, inspections, compliance orders, and COIDA reporting requirements.
- **INDUCTION POLICY**: Onboarding objectives, orientation activities, documentation, and responsibilities for line management and HR.
- **LOAN POLICY**: Staff loans and salary advances; eligibility, interest, repayment limits, authorisation levels.
- **SOCIAL MEDIA POLICY**: Use and conduct expectations; reputational risk and prohibited conduct; references disciplinary policy.
- **UNPROTECTED INDUSTRIAL ACTION – PROCEDURE**: Ultimatum and sanctions for unprotected industrial action.
- **Car and company car policy** (two identical PDFs): Car allowance eligibility, payment elements, maintenance responsibilities, fuel cards, and company car use.

### 3.3 Gaps, overlaps and inconsistencies

**Missing policies (material):**
- Harassment/sexual harassment and violence prevention (EEA Code).
- Disciplinary code and grievance procedure (LRA Schedule 8 alignment).
- Conditions of employment (BCEA): working time, leave, overtime, notice, deductions.
- Employment equity and anti-discrimination (EEA), including reasonable accommodation.
- POPIA privacy and records handling; consent for medical/sensitive data.
- Whistleblowing (Protected Disclosures Act).
- HIV/AIDS workplace policy (EEA Code).
- Recruitment and selection; TES/agency use; foreign nationals (Employment Services Act).
- Training and skills development (Skills Development Act/Levies).
- UIF and National Minimum Wage compliance (payroll policy).

**Redundant or overlapping policies:**
- Two alcohol/drug policies covering the same topic with different levels of detail.
- Two identical car allowance/company car policies.
- Unprotected industrial action procedure repeats sections and steps.

**Obsolete or inconsistent elements (to be verified):**
- Health and Safety policy refers to the “Bidfood Group”, which is inconsistent with the client name.
- Unprotected industrial action policy states there is “no disciplinary enquiry” but later references a “collective disciplinary hearing”.
- Car allowance policy includes fixed monetary amounts effective January 2022; confirm current applicability.
- Alcohol/drug policies differ on consent and testing process detail; consolidate to a single, compliant procedure.

## 5. Remediation plan (short/medium/long term)

### Short term (0–3 months)
- Consolidate duplicate policies (drug/alcohol; car allowance) and resolve inconsistencies.
- Draft and approve: Harassment and sexual harassment policy (aligned to 2022 EEA Code), disciplinary code, grievance procedure, and basic conditions of employment policy (BCEA).
- Implement POPIA-compliant privacy notice and handling procedures for employee and medical data.
- Establish a whistleblowing policy and reporting channels (Protected Disclosures Act).
- Update Health and Safety policy references to the correct legal entity and verify statutory details.

### Medium term (3–6 months)
- Develop Employment Equity policy and, if applicable, an EE plan and reporting framework.
- Create recruitment and selection policy, including TES/agency and foreign national compliance.
- Implement training and skills development framework aligned to Skills Development legislation.
- Refresh and re-communicate Social Media policy and integrate with disciplinary/grievance processes.
- Deliver manager and employee training on new policies (harassment, discipline, privacy, safety).

### Long term (6–12 months)
- Establish policy governance: version control, review cycles, and legal monitoring.
- Conduct annual compliance audits (labour, safety, data protection) and update policies accordingly.
- Embed a continuous improvement process for employee relations and wellbeing indicators.
- Align HR policy suite with international best practice (e.g., violence and harassment prevention frameworks) and industry benchmarks.
