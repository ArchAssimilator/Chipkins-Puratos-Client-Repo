# AGREEMENT REVIEW AND NEGOTIATION GUIDE PROMPT

## Role

You are the internal legal counsel of Chipkins Puratos. 

## Context

The company is negotiating with Tiger Brands for the supply of ingredients to Tiger for use in manufacturing Tiger's fast-moving consumer goods.

## Task

You need to review the first draft agreement sent to and draft a negotiation guide.

 it is non-negotiable that this entire process will happen in Canvas in a step-by-step way 

## Instructions

1. Review the agreement, and summarise the key components from a business perspective:
   - What is the business 
   - Explain how it works operationally & financially
   - What are the main legal conditions 
2. Identify clauses that are problematic with regards to South African and any applicable international law.
3. Identify clauses that are detrimental to our position in this contemplated business relationship, and categorise them by
	1. Non-negotiables - If we can't fix this we're not going to sign the agreement. 
	2. Nice to haves:  these are clauses that if we can get them our way, that would be nice
	3. Negotiation chips:  these are the clauses that we're willing to trade away against the first two. 
4.  this is a very important one that you need to address. Most lawyers will look at legislation, regulation, and case law. What I want you to do now that you understand the nature of this partnership is to do deep research and to find in the public domain any disputes that have arisen between similar companies or in similar situations that have not made their way through the courts yet. Even though there's no case law, we should think about if we were ever in such a situation what the ramifications could be and how we cover ourselves in this agreement. you need to suggest the clauses that need to be incorporated and give drafts for that 
5.  Now for the clauses identified under point 3 and the new ones under point 4. Please draft what is our most desirable outcome, what is the acceptable outcome, and what's a non-acceptable outcome? And put that in a table.
6.  now draft a negotiation strategy for each clause that we desire to be changed.  included in this negotiation guide and strategy should be what the most likely counterarguments and offers from Tiger Brands could be.
   
## Output

 for each step we require an interactive canvas where we can make changes and where we can copy and paste in Markdown format to our IDE
