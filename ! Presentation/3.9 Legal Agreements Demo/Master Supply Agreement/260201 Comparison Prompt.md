# Master Supply Agreement Comparison Analysis

## Role

Legal agreements analyst specialising in contract negotiation analysis and change documentation.

## Context

We are demonstrating generative AI's capability to perform sophisticated legal analysis. Specifically, we are analysing the Chipkins Puratos Master Supply Agreement negotiation process by comparing the original agreement presented to the counterparty against the final executed version.

**Original Document (Initial Offer)**
- File: `Presentation/3.9 Legal Agreements Demo/Master Supply Agreement/18.04.2023 CHIPKINS MASTER SUPPLY AGREEMENT.docx`

**Final Executed Agreement (Signed)**
- File: `Presentation/3.9 Legal Agreements Demo/Master Supply Agreement/Complete_with_Docusign_Chipkins_Master_Suppl-1.pdf`

## Task

Analyse and document all material changes between the original and final agreements, identifying the negotiating positions and outcomes for each party.

## Instructions

1. **Extract and compare** both agreements, identifying all changes (deletions, additions, modifications to language)
2. **Categorize changes** by clause type (e.g., payment terms, liability, indemnification, termination, confidentiality, etc.)
3. **Infer negotiation positions**: For each change, explain:
   - Who likely advocated for this change (based on who benefits)
   - Whether this represents a win, loss, or compromise for Chipkins Puratos
   - The likely business driver for each position
4. **Assess outcomes** by determining who succeeded on each issue and at what cost (trade-offs)

## Output Format

A structured table with the following columns:

| Clause/Section | Original Language | Final Language | Negotiation Outcome | Business Impact for Chipkins | Analysis |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| *[Clause name/number]* | *[Original provision]* | *[Final provision]* | *[Party positions & result]* | *[Risk/benefit assessment]* | *[Context for change]* |

**Requirements:**
- Focus on substantive changes (ignore formatting, numbering, or grammatical corrections)
- For each change, clearly indicate which party achieved their preferred position
- Identify trade-offs: what Chipkins conceded to gain other improvements
- Format analysis to be concise yet comprehensive—suitable for presenting to executives unfamiliar with the contract
