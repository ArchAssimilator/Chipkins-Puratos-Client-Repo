# Master Supply Agreement — Negotiation Comparison

## Executive Summary

The final executed agreement (signed April–May 2025) represents a substantially renegotiated contract from the original April 2023 draft. Chipkins Puratos secured major wins on contract duration (5 years vs 24 months), total requirements commitment, and removal of Tiger's unilateral exit rights. Tiger gained concessions on equipment purchase flexibility, loss-of-profits exclusions, faster defect remediation, price transparency, and weekly invoicing. The deal reflects a mature, balanced commercial relationship where Chipkins committed significant capital (~R9.8M in equipment) in exchange for long-term volume certainty.

## Key Trade-Off Summary

**What Chipkins Won:**
- 5-year term (vs 24 months)
- Total requirements commitment (vs discretionary ordering)
- Removal of Tiger's unilateral termination rights
- Loss of profits liability cap
- UPS/Generator infrastructure obligation on Tiger
- Volume-linked equipment protections
- Change of ownership consent shifted to Supplier
- Expanded scope (added Improvers)
- Exit management protections (cl 23)

**What Chipkins Conceded:**
- Equipment purchase converted from obligation to option for Tiger
- 1 business day defect replacement (vs 5 days)
- Full cost transparency and CPI-1% price constraint
- Weekly invoicing (vs monthly)
- ~R9.8M capital investment in on-site equipment
- Per-Premises termination right for Tiger (90 days)

**Overall Assessment:** Chipkins achieved a strategically favourable outcome. The concessions on equipment flexibility and pricing transparency were the price paid for the two most valuable provisions: a 5-year term and total requirements commitment. These provisions, combined with the R9.8M equipment investment, create substantial switching costs for Tiger and position Chipkins as an embedded, long-term supply partner rather than a transactional vendor.
