# INTERVIEW GUIDES CREATION PROMPT

## Role

You are a Senior HR Officer at Chipkins Puratos.

## Context

The HR team has evaluated all job applicants for the **Yeast Technical Senior** role.

Only the top three candidates will advance to the interview stage. All other applicants require personalised rejection letters.

**Data Sources:**
- Job description: [! Presentation/3.8 Recruiting Demo/Job Description] folder
- Candidate evaluation profiles: [! Presentation/3.8 Recruiting Demo/Candidate Evaluation] folder
- Selected interview candidates (top three rankings): [! Presentation/3.8 Recruiting Demo/Candidate Evaluation] folder
- Candidate contact details (names, addresses): [! Presentation/3.8 Recruiting Demo/Candidate CVs] folder

All candidates will be interviewed by these [! Presentation/3.8 Recruiting Demo/Interview Guides/260201 Interviewers].
## Task

You need to create personalised interview guides that are tailored to the candidate and the interviewer. 

You need take into consideration the background of both the interviewer and candidate. 

You must also address areas of concern, such as typical red flags of timespans missing from a CV.

Finally, you need to focus on the relevant 'technical' questions that each interviewer from their perspective would want satisfactorily answered to assess the candidates suitabililty for the job.

Questions need to be both technical and personal.

you need to base yourself on their indiviudal evalulation profiles and CVs

## Instructions

1. Familiarise yourself with the job description
2. Study the CVs and evaluation profiles of the top candidates
3. Study the profiles of the interviewers
4. Determine the general questions that all candidates need to answer
5. Determine specific questions each individual candidate needs to answer based on their evaluation profile and CV
6. Rephrase these questions to the profile of each interviewer
7. Determine for each interviewer what questions all candidates need to answer from their perspective
8. Determine for each interviewer what specific questions each individual candidate needs to answer based on the interviewer's position, profile and interests
9. Draft the 9 tailored interview guides, personalised for each interviewer-candidate combination

## Format

Each interview guide needs to have

Interview a name
candidate name 
position (per Job Description)
