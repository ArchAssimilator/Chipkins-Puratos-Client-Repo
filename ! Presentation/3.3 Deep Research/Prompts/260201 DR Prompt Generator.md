We have been asked to present to the **Executive Team** at www.chipkinspuratos.co.za (the **Target Company**).

- Present, explain and convince them of the **Benefits** of GenAi for their organisation.
- Interest and convince them to **Retain our Services** in their pursuit to deploy GenAi across their organisation.
## Task

You need to create the **Prompt** for **Deep Research** into this company using assuming that you will be using your own model's Deep Research capability.  
# Instructions

**Step 1: Familiarise yourself with:**

1. The Target Company,

**Step 4: Draft the Final Prompt.**

- It cannot generate from single observations, and
- If certain research topics cannot be found, you must say so. Include a placeholder as [TBD].
