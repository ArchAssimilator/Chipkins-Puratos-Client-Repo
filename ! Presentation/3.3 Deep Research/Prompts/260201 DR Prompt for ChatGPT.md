# Chipkin Puratos Deep Research Prompt (ChatGPT)

## Role

You are a senior research analyst using ChatGPT’s built‑in Deep Research capability.

## Context

We are preparing for a presentation to the Executive Team at www.chipkinspuratos.co.za (the Target Company). The meeting objectives are:

- Present, explain, and convince them of the benefits of GenAI for their organisation.
- Build interest and convince them to retain our services to deploy GenAI across their organisation.

The research must be comprehensive, reliable, and defensible to executive scrutiny.

## Task

Produce a company Deep Research report on Chipkins Puratos, strictly following the structure and section headings of the **Company Deep Research Report Template**. Use only reputable, trusted sources and ensure every factual claim is supported by evidence.

## Instructions

1. **Plan the research** before writing. Identify the key sources you will consult for each section and validate information across multiple sources.
2. **Use Deep Research** to gather information from authoritative sources (e.g., company site, official filings, reputable industry and financial publications, government or regulatory bodies, credible news outlets, and recognised market research firms).
3. **No hallucinations**: if information cannot be found or verified, state this clearly and include a placeholder as `[TBD]`.
4. **Avoid single‑source conclusions**: do not rely on a single observation for any material point; corroborate with multiple sources where possible.
5. **Focus on decision‑useful insights** relevant to GenAI opportunity framing, adoption readiness, and strategic value for the Target Company.
6. **Citations**: include clear source citations for all non‑trivial facts and claims.
7. **Formatting**: output the final report in **markdown inside a single code block**, following the template exactly, with the same section order and headings.
8. **Language**: use UK English only.
