# CHIPKINS PURATOS LABOUR LAW QUESTIONS

1.  Are our contract specialists and freelance formulation consultants who invoice us and work for competitors true independent contractors under *Denel v Gerber*, or do we face misclassification exposure as employees?

2.  Our warehouse night-shift teams finish at 23:30 with stock-taking; what BCEA night-work premiums, rest periods, health surveillance, and sector rules apply under the General Wage Order?

3.  May we extend a production scheduler's hours during peak demand season without breaching BCEA rest, meal, and fatigue obligations—and what documentation and advance notice is required?

4.  A factory floor worker posts a photo on Instagram showing food safety shortcuts; how do we handle a disciplinary investigation while protecting the company and surviving CCMA scrutiny?

5.  Our warehouse team logged 48 hours last week including work on a public holiday; how much additional overtime is permissible this week, what consent or averaging arrangements are needed, and what must be in writing?

6.  A logistics contractor damaged R50k worth of raw ingredients through careless handling but earns R22k/month; under BCEA Section 34 and *Shoprite v CCMA*, what deductions or recovery options do we have?

7.  What contractual language can we use to properly classify specialist contractors (e.g., food technologists, recipe developers) while satisfying SARS, CCMA substance-over-form tests, and compliance auditor expectations?

8.  One production team member requests Fridays off for religious observance and another needs time for cultural ceremonies; how do we balance accommodation requests against operational demands per *FAWU v Rainbow Chicken*?

9.  A 10-year senior production supervisor made an error in batch documentation that risked food safety compliance; how do seniority, potential disciplinary alternatives, and the *Sidumo* fairness test affect dismissal decisions?

10. Our G20 supply contract with a major customer ends in seven weeks and we need to reduce the fixed-term production team from ten staff (including two pregnant workers, one union shop steward, and several repeat-hire contractors); what Section 189 consultation duties and retrenchment process apply under *Enforce Security v Fikile*?
