# Comparison — Codex Summary vs Microsoft Teams Summary

## Scope and detail coverage
- Codex summary is a concise, chronological flow with explicit action items for both parties.
- MST summary is thematic (sectioned by topic) and covers a broader range of business/IT topics.

## Topics present in MST but missing or lightly covered in Codex
- R&D workflow support (Remy’s process, report automation, knowledge base).
- Marketing/social media automation and agency costs.
- IT systems landscape and integrations (SysPro ERP, DataScope WMS, RightScan, Microsoft stack, Data Smith/FMCGX).
- Security/privacy considerations, including local models for highly confidential data.
- AI platform comparison (OpenAI/Claude/Gemini/Grok in Microsoft context; Claude in Excel).
- External data value vs internal data focus.
- Participant engagement/Q&A structure and explicit refresher-course option.

## Topics present in Codex but missing in MST
- Early call logistics (audio/video issues resolved by turning video off).
- Weekend availability for questions and a Monday follow‑up.
- Emphasis on fixing masterclass dates due to availability (booking/lock‑in language).

## Action items comparison
- Codex includes actions for both Rutger‑Jan and Carl.
- MST lists only Carl’s tasks; no explicit Rutger‑Jan follow‑ups.

## Overall takeaway
- Codex is tighter and action‑oriented but omits several operational/IT and business use‑case details captured by MST.
- MST is broader and more detailed but less chronological and lighter on bilateral action ownership.
