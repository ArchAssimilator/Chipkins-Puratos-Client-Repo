## Company Overview

### Business Model
Chipkins Puratos manufactures and distributes bakery ingredients across southern Africa:
- **Products:** Bread/roll premixes, confectionery premixes, cake mixes (Satin range), bread improvers (Volumat), fruit fillings (Topfil), and specialty raw materials
- **Customers:** Artisanal bakeries, commercial bakeries, retailers, food service
- **Headquarters:** Edenvale, Johannesburg

### Parent Company Relationship
- **Puratos (Belgium):** Global bakery/patisserie/chocolate ingredients company, 8,700+ employees, presence in 100+ countries, founded 1919
- **JV Structure:** Equal partnership announced between Puratos and Bidcorp Food's Bakery Solutions Division to jointly develop bakery business in southern Africa
- **Strategic Direction:** Plans to increase local production and bring more value-added ingredients to African bakers

### Market Position
- Largest bakery ingredients distributor in South Africa
- Nearly 100 years of local market expertise (via Bidcorp heritage)
- Access to Puratos's global R&D, product innovation, and quality standards

## Industry Pain Points

### 1. Commodity Price Volatility
- Wheat stocks declining (32.1% stocks-to-use ratio projected)
- Cocoa prices surged ~300% in past year
- Egg prices at record highs (avian flu impact)
- **GenAI Opportunity:** Predictive pricing models, hedging strategy optimization

### 2. Supply Chain Fragility
- Cold-chain logistics disruptions
- Inconsistent supplier quality in organic/specialty ingredients
- JV integration complexity (Puratos global + Bidcorp local)
- **GenAI Opportunity:** Demand forecasting, supplier risk monitoring, inventory optimization

### 3. Customer Relationship Complexity
- Diverse customer base (small artisanal to large commercial)
- Price sensitivity in inflationary environment
- Technical support requirements (bakery formulation assistance)
- **GenAI Opportunity:** Customer segmentation, churn prediction, AI-assisted technical support

### 4. Regulatory & Compliance Burden
- Food safety and labeling standards (clean-label trends)
- FSSC 22000 / HACCP requirements
- **GenAI Opportunity:** Automated compliance monitoring, document generation

### 5. Labor Challenges
- Skilled worker shortages across food manufacturing
- Training new staff on complex formulations
- **GenAI Opportunity:** AI-powered training materials, knowledge capture from experienced staff
