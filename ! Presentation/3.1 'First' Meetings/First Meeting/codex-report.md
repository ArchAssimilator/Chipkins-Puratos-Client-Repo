# Chipkins Puratos Meeting Prep Brief

## Executive Summary
- Finance-led attendees will respond best to quantified savings, cashflow impact, and risk reduction; keep proposals tied to measurable outcomes.
- Position GenAi.StudioLAB as a fast path to de-risked pilots: short workshops, prioritized use cases, and rapid proof-of-value.
- Target near-term wins in finance ops, commercial insights, and customer service where data is already available.
- Use medium and strategic initiatives to show a roadmap, but keep the focus on phased ROI.

## Company Overview
- **Business model:** Chipkins Puratos distributes bakery, patisserie, and chocolate ingredients and supplies to industrial and artisanal bakeries, QSRs, retail bakeries, and food manufacturers. Revenue likely driven by product sales, technical support, and innovation partnerships.
- **Key products (typical portfolio):** bakery mixes and improvers, enzymes, fillings, glazes, chocolate and cocoa-based products, emulsifiers, and specialty ingredients.
- **Market position (SA):** Well-established ingredient supplier with access to Puratos global R&D, product innovation, and technical know-how.
- **Parent relationship:** Part of the Puratos Group (Belgium), which provides product platforms, R&D, and likely shared systems/standards.

## Industry Pain Points (Bakery Ingredients Suppliers)
- **Demand volatility:** seasonal and promotional spikes make forecasting and production planning difficult.
- **Margin pressure:** fluctuating commodity prices, FX exposure, and energy/logistics costs.
- **Complex pricing:** varied customer tiers, rebates, promotions, and contract terms.
- **Inventory risk:** shelf-life constraints, write-offs, and slow-moving SKUs.
- **Service expectations:** high-touch technical support and rapid response times.
- **Regulatory compliance:** food safety, labeling, traceability, and audit readiness.

## Recommended GenAI Initiatives

### Quick Wins (0-3 months)
1. **Finance Close Copilot**
   - Automate variance commentary drafts from GL + operational data.
   - **ROI:** reduce close cycle time and analyst hours.
2. **Customer Query Triage**
   - AI-assisted email/Teams responses for order status, delivery ETA, and common product questions.
   - **ROI:** faster response times and lower support workload.
3. **Sales Margin Insights Digest**
   - Weekly auto-generated brief showing margin drift by customer/SKU, with recommended actions.
   - **ROI:** immediate visibility into leakage and discounting.

### Medium-term (3-12 months)
1. **Demand Sensing + Forecast Assist**
   - Blend sales history, promo calendars, and external signals to improve forecasts.
   - **ROI:** better stock availability and reduced write-offs.
2. **Price/Promotion Effectiveness Analyzer**
   - Summarize promo ROI and elasticity by segment.
   - **ROI:** stronger pricing discipline and improved margin.
3. **Procurement & Supplier Risk Monitor**
   - Summarize supplier performance, lead-time risk, and contract compliance.
   - **ROI:** fewer supply disruptions, better negotiating leverage.

### Strategic (12+ months)
1. **End-to-End Cost-to-Serve Optimization**
   - Model profitability by customer, route, and SKU; recommend service-level changes.
   - **ROI:** structural margin improvement and better customer segmentation.
2. **R&D/Innovation Intelligence Hub**
   - Mine trends, feedback, and competitor signals to prioritize product development.
   - **ROI:** faster innovation cycles and higher win rates.
3. **Integrated Decision Cockpit**
   - Role-based GenAI layer over ERP/BI for finance and ops leaders.
   - **ROI:** faster decisions and reduced dependency on analyst teams.

## Discovery Questions
1. Where do you see the most manual effort in finance or reporting today?
2. What are your largest sources of margin leakage (discounting, waste, logistics, pricing)?
3. How confident are you in your demand forecasts and inventory decisions?
4. Which customer service or technical support queries consume the most time?
5. What data systems are you using (ERP, CRM, BI), and how accessible is the data?
6. What would a 90-day proof-of-value need to deliver to justify scaling?
7. Are there any compliance or audit pain points that slow down operations?

## Potential Objections & Responses
- **Objection:** “AI sounds interesting, but ROI is unclear.”
  - **Response:** We start with 2–3 use cases tied to measurable KPIs (close time, margin leakage, write-offs) and run a short proof-of-value with clear payback criteria.
- **Objection:** “We don’t have the data in good shape.”
  - **Response:** The StudioLAB phase includes data readiness assessment; quick wins are chosen to leverage what is already accessible.
- **Objection:** “We’re concerned about data privacy and compliance.”
  - **Response:** We implement enterprise-grade controls and can run solutions in a private environment aligned to Puratos/Chipkins policies.
- **Objection:** “This feels like a big transformation.”
  - **Response:** We focus on a phased roadmap: quick wins first, then scale based on proven impact.
