## Recommended Presentation Strategy

### 1. Open with Business Value (for Nigel Phillips)
Start with enterprise-wide strategic benefits: competitive advantage, workforce empowerment (not replacement), and alignment with Chipkins' values.

### 2. Address Technical Feasibility Early (for Martin van Staden & Tahlia Elcome)
Don't leave technical questions unanswered. Proactively cover: integration approach, security, data governance, and scalability. These two can make or break adoption.

### 3. Show Function-Specific Use Cases
Have ready examples for each executive function — don't be generic. Each Exco member will want to see relevance to their domain.

### 4. Position as Pilot with Scale Potential
Martin van Staden's presence from BCFA signals group interest. Position any initiative as a pilot that could scale across Bidcorp Food Africa if successful.

### 5. Emphasise Workforce Augmentation
Given Nigel's Bakery School commitment and Nomsa's HR leadership, frame AI as empowering employees, not replacing them. This is culturally important.
