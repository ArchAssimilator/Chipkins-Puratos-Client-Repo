V-CARD ENRICHMENT PROMPT

Objective

Enhance each individual vCard by researching and appending authoritative, up-to-date information from reliable online sources.

Instructions

For each vCard, perform the following steps:
	1.	Online Research
Conduct targeted online research to identify verifiable information related to the individual. Prioritise primary or reputable secondary sources (official websites, institutional profiles, professional networks, published works).
	2.	Augment vCard Fields
Where available, add or update the following details:
	•	Current job title and employer
	•	Work address (physical and postal, if different)
	•	Office telephone numbers (direct line and/or switchboard)
	3.	Notes Section – Professional Profile
In the Notes field, create a concise but informative profile that may include:
	•	Educational background
	•	Previous employment history
	•	Publications, presentations, or notable projects
	•	Professional affiliations or significant relationships
	•	Areas of expertise or interests relevant to future interactions
	•	Any contextual insights that would support informed engagement
	4.	Sources and URLs
Include direct URLs to all external sources used, clearly attributed, so the information can be verified or revisited later.

Quality Guidelines
	•	Ensure accuracy and relevance; do not speculate.
	•	Clearly distinguish confirmed facts from contextual information.
	•	Keep the tone factual, neutral, and professional.
	•	If information cannot be found, leave the field unchanged and note the gap where appropriate.
